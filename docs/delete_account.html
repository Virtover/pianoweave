Delete your Piano Weave account

You can delete your account and data here at any time. This page runs in your browser and talks directly to Google Drive and the Piano Weave server.

What gets deleted

  • Server account: your Google account ID, email address, transcription minute balance and transcription jobs. Unused minutes cannot be restored.
  • Cloud library: songs the app synced to the hidden Piano Weave folder in your Google Drive. Your other Drive files are not touched.
  • Kept: purchase records, without your account ID or email, so a purchase cannot be credited twice. Encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
  • On your phone: not reachable from here. Uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
