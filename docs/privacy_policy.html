Piano Weave – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Piano Weave Android app (package com.lumenchord.pianoweave, the "App") and the Piano Weave transcription server (the "Server") collect, how it is used, and what choices you have. The App and the Server are developed and operated by Krzysztof Olszak ("I", "me", "the developer").

In short: the microphone is used only to detect the notes you play, and that audio is processed on your device. Your MIDI library stays on your device unless you turn on cloud sync. If you transcribe a video or sign in with Google, the information listed below is sent to the Server. I do not sell your data and the App does not show ads.

Information collected automatically by the Server

Like most online services, the Server and its hosting infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, request time, request type and error messages. This is used to operate the Server, prevent abuse and fix problems.

Information we do not collect

  • The App does not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • The App does not read your contacts, messages or photos.
  • The App does not record or upload audio from your microphone (see Microphone).
  • The App does not contain advertising or advertising identifiers.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the App's features: transcribing videos into MIDI, storing and syncing your library, and playing practice sessions.
  • To sign you in, keep your transcription balance, apply free minutes and process purchases.
  • To protect the Server from abuse, fraud and misuse.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the App and the Server, and to answer support requests.
  • To keep backups of the billing database so that purchased minutes are not lost.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), I rely on performance of a contract (providing the features you request), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention and backups), consent (microphone and Google Drive access, which you can withdraw at any time) and legal obligation.

3. Microphone

Wait Mode uses the microphone to listen to your piano and pause the sheet music until you play the correct notes. The App asks for the microphone permission only when you use this feature. Audio is analyzed in real time on your device by a model that runs locally. The audio is not saved and is not sent to the Server or to anyone else. You can revoke the permission at any time in your Android settings; the rest of the App continues to work.

4. Sharing and third parties

I do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as follows:

  • Google. The App uses Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing and, if you enable cloud sync, the Google Drive API. The Server verifies your Google access token and your purchases with Google. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Video and audio websites. To transcribe a link, the Server requests the audio from the website that hosts it (for example YouTube). That website sees a request coming from the Server's IP address, not from your device, and handles it under its own policies.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers. The Server runs on infrastructure that processes data on my behalf, including backups. Backups are encrypted before they are stored outside the Server.
  • Legal reasons. I may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and the security of the service.

The App and this website may contain links to third-party services, such as community, donation or membership platforms (for example Discord, Ko-fi or Patreon). If you follow such a link or donate, that service collects information under its own policies; I am not responsible for them.

5. Retention

  • Files created during a transcription (downloaded audio and generated MIDI) are temporary on the Server and are removed automatically shortly after the job finishes.
  • Account and billing records (Google account ID, email address, balance, purchases) are kept while your account is active and as needed to handle purchases, refunds and disputes, or as required by law.
  • Backups of the billing database are kept for a limited period and then replaced.
  • On your device, songs you delete in the App stay in a recycle state for up to 30 days and are then permanently removed. Uninstalling the App deletes its local data.
  • In Google Drive, synced files stay until you delete them in the App, turn off and remove them, or delete them from Drive yourself.

6. Security

Data between the App and the Server is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) when the Server is configured with a secure address. Access to the billing database is limited, and its backups are encrypted when copied off the Server. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Sign out in the App to remove the stored Google sign-in from your device.
  • Cloud sync is optional and off by default. You can turn it off at any time.
  • Permissions. You can revoke microphone access in Android settings, and Google Drive access in your Google Account settings.
  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the UK GDPR), you may have the right to access the data held about you, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact me (see Contact). You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Deleting your account and data

Delete your account yourself

You can delete your Piano Weave account and data at any time, without contacting me:

  1. Open the account deletion page. In the App, it is linked from Settings → About → Delete account & data.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account you use in the App.
  3. Choose what to delete and confirm.
DataWhat happens
Server account (Google account ID, email address, minute balance, transcription jobs and their files) Deleted from the Server immediately. Copies in encrypted backups disappear when those backups expire (within 30 days).
Purchase records Kept without your account ID or email address, so that the same purchase cannot be credited twice.
Free-minute timer If you received free minutes recently, a one-way hash of your account ID and the time of the last grant is kept until the current free-minute period ends, so that deleting and re-creating an account cannot be used to collect extra free minutes.
Cloud library (the App's files in the hidden app folder of your Google Drive) Deleted immediately. Your other Google Drive files are not accessed or changed.
Data on your device (library, settings, sign-in) Not reachable from the web. Uninstall the App, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Piano Weave → Storage.
  • Unused transcription minutes are removed together with the account and cannot be restored.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel or refund past purchases. Refunds are handled through Google Play.
  • After deletion, you can revoke the App's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Ask me to delete it

If you cannot use the deletion page, email me from the address linked to your Google account (see Contact) and I will delete your Server account within 30 days.

9. Other servers

The App can be configured to use a different transcription server. The App never sends your Google sign-in token to such a server; it identifies you only with the random device identifier, and in-app purchases are not available there. If you connect to a server that I do not operate, that server's operator, not me, decides what data is collected (for example the video links you submit and your IP address) and how it is handled. This Policy applies only to the Server that I operate.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable local law sets a higher age), and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. If a change is significant, I will notify you in the App or on the App's store page.
