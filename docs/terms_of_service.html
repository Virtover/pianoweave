2. What the App does

  • Practice: shows MIDI songs as falling notes, plays them back and, in Wait Mode, waits for you to play the right notes.
  • Transcription: the Server turns a video link, an audio or video file, or a recording you make in the App into a MIDI file.
  • Processing capacity: when the Server is busy, I may run a transcription on GPU capacity from a third-party provider. This doesn't change the price, the credits used or the result. See the Privacy Policy for what data is sent.
  • Library and cloud sync: keeps your songs on your device and, if you turn it on, backs them up to your own Google Drive.
  • Other servers: you can connect the App to a transcription server run by someone else. That server's operator, not me, is responsible for it.

The App itself is free. Transcription on the official Server may be free, or paid with credits with some free minutes each month, and this can change over time. Before you start, the App shows whether a transcription uses credits and how many (see Credits and purchases).
